--- STYLE
Clean electro-pop with laboratory textures at 112 BPM in E minor, sung in English. A female vocalist sings cool, crisp and precise in the verses, almost spoken like a scientist reading data, then bright, wide and sustained in the chorus with airy breathy ad-libs between the lines. A pulsing analog bass, a tight electronic kick, crisp glass-like hi-hats and a bright arpeggio carry the verses, tiny test-tube clink samples sparkle between phrases, and the chorus adds a wide supersaw pad, a punchy clap and layered vocal harmonies. Curious, awed mood about carbon, the element in every leaf, every diamond and every breath. Ends on keyboard clicks fading out one by one. Polished, clear, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, heavy distortion, metal, country, acoustic folk, screaming, rap verses, comedy, orchestral strings
