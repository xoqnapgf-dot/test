--- STYLE
Explosive Japanese girl-band hard rock with a church-and-shrine theme at 168 BPM in E minor, sung in Japanese. A female lead vocalist belts with a powerful, bright, slightly raw voice, steady in the verses, then soaring in the chorus over layered girl-group harmonies that shout the chorus words together. A palm-muted rhythm guitar, a heavy locked-in bass and double-bass drumming carry the verses, a keyboard plays a holy melody, and the chorus adds a twin guitar wall, crowd claps, church bell hits and a gong. Fierce, joyful, sacred-rebellious mood of five names becoming one sound, where the chorus erases every sect and a girl's voice becomes a blade. Ends with the last drum hit and a bell ringing out. Loud, wide, vocal-forward live-band mix.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, English vocals, ballad, acoustic folk, country, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, orchestral strings
