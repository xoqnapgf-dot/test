--- STYLE
Warm Japanese indie-folk pop at 82 BPM in D major, sung in Japanese. A soft female vocalist sings tender and breathy, close to the microphone in the verses, then clear, emotional and soaring in the chorus with warm harmonies above. A fingerpicked acoustic guitar, a warm melodic bass and a soft cajon carry the verses, a violin plays long notes and answers the vocal phrases, a distant foghorn sounds between sections, and the final chorus opens into a full band with a key change. Nostalgic, bittersweet mood of a foggy harbor town, a doughnut split in half, and a lost friend's bass note echoing across the sea. Ends on the guitar fading with violin harmonics, waves and a distant foghorn. Warm, natural, vocal-forward mix.
