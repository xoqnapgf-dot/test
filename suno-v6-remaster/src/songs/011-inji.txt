--- STYLE
Warm Mandopop acoustic ballad at 72 BPM in G major, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male tenor sings soft and sincere, close to the microphone in the verses, then opening into a clear, bittersweet and sustained chorus with a touch of breath at the ends of phrases. A fingerpicked nylon guitar and a soft felt piano carry the verses, a muted kick and warm upright bass enter in the bridge, and the chorus adds strings, brushed drums and a gentle electric guitar echo. Nostalgic, tender mood of a glance that left a mark on a whole life, longing like a rainbow after rain scattering in the wind. Ends on the guitar alone with a long reverb tail. Vocal-forward, natural warm mix.
