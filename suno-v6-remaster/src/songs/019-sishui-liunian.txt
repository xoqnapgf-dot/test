--- STYLE
Nostalgic Mandopop folk-rock at 76 BPM in G major, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male voice sings warm, slightly husky and honest, close to the microphone and almost spoken in the verses, then opening into a wide, aching and sustained chorus with long held line endings. A fingerpicked acoustic guitar and a soft piano carry the verses, a harmonica answers between phrases, a warm bass and brushed drums enter in the second verse, and the chorus adds strummed guitars, strings, big reverb drums and layered backing harmonies. Wistful, homesick mood of a musician remembering lost years, fireflies in a forest and a guitar that leads him back home. Ends on the guitar alone with one harmonica note. Vocal-forward, natural warm mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, rap, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, heavy distortion, screaming, autotune, female lead vocal

[Intro | 8 bars, fingerpicked acoustic guitar alone, a soft piano joins on bar five, a lonely harmonica phrase on the last two bars, instrumental only]

[Verse 1 | part 1 of 3, warm husky male voice, close-mic, guitar and piano only, one breath per line, slightly behind the beat]
再写不出那样的歌曲，
心灵与人生的共鸣似已离去，
再遇不见那场迷离双眼的大雨，
可为何那份模糊仍在我身前延续。

[Verse 1 | part 2 of 3, same voice, a little warmer, harmonica answers after each pair of lines]
我梦到过刻骨铭心的爱恋，
许下的承诺直达世界的终焉，
我唱过青葱岁月峥嵘的理想，
午夜孤泣的吉他领我重回故乡。

[Verse 1 | part 3 of 3, voice lifting gently, warm bass enters, soft brushed drums begin]
我偶遇过亿万萤火虫漂泊的森林，
它们是黑暗中摇曳无定的光明，
我弹奏过彩虹编织的琴弦，
白云跳着舞蹦到我面前。

[Break | band stops, one beat of silence, singer breathes in]

看过的重现吧，听过的回荡啊，
带我引我回到那个家。

[Verse 2 | part 1 of 3, back to the close honest voice, guitar and piano, bass and brushed drums stay, a little more weary]
再记不起当初的那张容颜，
炽热的情意似乎早已变迁，
再无法回到的那些年，
他们是否仍孤守在岁月的起点。

[Verse 2 | part 2 of 3, more inward, almost spoken, guitar alone with the harmonica]
我听过深夜时分自己灵魂的呢喃，
矛盾与执念在心底最深处纠缠，
我写过的情愫如浪起时的波澜，
拍打在岸边最终只得轻叹。

[Verse 2 | part 3 of 3, voice rising with pain, drums return, strings swell slowly]
我镌刻过恒久持续的不甘，
再度抚摸我的手指仍旧莫名发颤，
我品味过枯萎的白花一瓣又一瓣，
