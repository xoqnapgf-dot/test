--- STYLE
Solemn cinematic Chinese-style (guofeng) post-rock at 78 BPM in B minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A female alto sings grave, clear and restrained in the verses, then wide, aching and sustained in the chorus with a faint choir behind her, and recites the bridge in an opera-style free tempo that starts slow and tightens. A plucked guzheng, a distant erhu and sparse low drums with a cold atmosphere open the song, a deep bass and a dark pad carry the verses, and the chorus adds tremolo guitars, big reverb drums, strings and a choir. Heavy, mournful and awestruck mood of a burning house of the world where five saints stand side by side asking who is in charge, and humanity chooses to be a witness. Ends on guzheng and erhu fading into a soft pad. Wide, cinematic, vocal-forward mix.
