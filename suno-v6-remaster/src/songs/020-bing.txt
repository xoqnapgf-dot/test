--- STYLE
Manic Chinese art-punk and alt-rock at 122 BPM in E minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist sings with a strained, urgent, slightly cracked voice, half-spoken and shaky in the verses, then wide, ragged and sustained in the chorus with gang vocals shouting the key word. Angular jangly guitars, a driving melodic bass and tight live drums carry the verses, a warped carnival-like organ wobbles in the background, and the chorus adds distorted power chords, crash cymbals and layered crowd shouts. Unhinged but tender mood of a person who sees upside-down skies, upside-down rainbows and his own double waving at him, the whole world sick. Ends on a single distorted chord and a faint laugh of feedback. Raw, punchy, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, rap, ballad, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, lo-fi, autotune, female lead vocal, orchestral strings
