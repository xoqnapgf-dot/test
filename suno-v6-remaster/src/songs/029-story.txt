--- STYLE
Alternative country-folk with a deadpan storytelling vocal at 112 BPM in G major, sung in English. A male singer tells the story in a dry, deadpan, slightly sardonic baritone in the verses, then sings the chorus lively and open, with a loose, slightly drunken-sounding backing group answering each line. A twangy fingerpicked acoustic guitar, a simple walking bass and a shuffling drum beat carry the verses, a lonesome harmonica plays short mournful phrases between lines, and the chorus adds a bright banjo, a weeping slide guitar and an upbeat country two-step groove. Absurd, comic and bittersweet mood of a story that cannot exist but happens all the time. Ends on one rousing final chord and a long wailing harmonica note. Warm, natural, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, heavy distortion, metal, rap, autotune, opera, synth pads
