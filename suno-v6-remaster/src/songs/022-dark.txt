--- STYLE
Gothic darkwave and post-punk at 74 BPM in C-sharp minor, sung in English. A deep male baritone sings in a cold, hollow, half-spoken voice in the verses, then rises to a wide, haunted and sustained chorus with a ghostly layered double behind him. A chorus-drenched clean guitar, a throbbing melodic bass and a slow mechanical drum machine carry the verses, a cold synth drone swells like a black hole, and the chorus adds huge reverb drums, a wall of tremolo guitars and a cathedral-like pad. Bleak, hopeless mood of an endless void swallowing the light, still longing for one flare of flame. Ends on the drone alone fading into silence. Spacious, dark, vocal-forward mix.
