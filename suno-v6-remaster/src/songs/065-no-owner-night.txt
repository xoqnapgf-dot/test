--- STYLE
Bass-led dark post-punk and darkwave at 118 BPM in F-sharp minor, sung in Japanese. A female vocalist sings icy and flat in the verses, deadpan and close to the microphone, then fuller and colder in the chorus with a faint double behind her, half-whispering in the bridge and gaining power in the final build. A heavy sub-bass plays the lead melody throughout, a motorik drum machine stays minimal, a cold synth pulse and a muted guitar stab give texture, an angular chorus-pedal clean guitar adds one solo, and the full band enters in the chorus. Cold, defiant mood of a number in a wired city choosing a frozen ownerless night over kneeling to anyone. Ends on the bass pulse and whispered vocals fading. Dark, wide, vocal-forward mix with a strong low end.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, English vocals, ballad, acoustic folk, country, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, orchestral strings
