Poetic Chinese-style (guofeng) chamber ballad at 66 BPM in D minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. [This is a Mandarin song]

[Intro | 8 bars, a plucked guqin alone, a breathy bamboo flute joins on bar five, a faint wind texture, instrumental only]

[Verse 1 | warm weary baritone, nearly spoken, guqin and guitar only, a breath after every line, slightly behind the beat]
你想为这天下，奏一曲新的乐章，
指尖下没有金戈铁马，只有麦浪与工商，
你说这古老的琴弦，太过紧绷也太过悲凉，
应弹出寻常巷陌里，人间的渴望。

[Pre-Chorus | voice a little firmer, cello and pad enter, thunder rumbles faintly in the distance]
可窗外雷声渐响，满座皆是旧日王孙，
他们说你的乐音，太过自由也太过天真，
你看见满座的犹疑，却只对自己虔诚。

[Chorus | wide aching sustained voice, strings and a soft kick enter, flute sings above, distant choir, line endings held long]

从此怀抱残破的琴，再无人听我说话，
我只是个凡人啊，却错生在了神话。

[Verse 2 | back to the nearly spoken baritone, guqin and cello, a little colder, the street outside the window]
你并未转身离去，也并未低下头颅，
只是将所有的言语，都还给了最初的孤独，
你看着窗外的世界，走上你画下的草图，
那上面繁花似锦，只是再与你毫无干系。

[Pre-Chorus | same as before, the thunder louder, strings tense]
