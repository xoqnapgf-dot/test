--- STYLE
Chinese rap-rock with an acoustic-to-electric guitar duel at 88 BPM in E minor, rapped and sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist raps in an earnest, hoarse, flowing voice with unhurried phrasing and a clear breath between phrases, then sings the chorus open and sustained with long held line endings. A fingerpicked acoustic guitar and a deep bass carry the first verse, a steady live kick and snare join, and the chorus adds a bright lead electric guitar that cuts like a sword, strummed power chords, strings and layered backing harmonies. Lonely, passionate mood of a musician whose guitar is a holy sword, homesick for faraway towns, a band that fell apart, and tears on an empty shore. Ends on the acoustic guitar alone with one ringing note. Vocal-forward, natural live-band mix.
