--- STYLE
Warm Mandopop indie-folk at 80 BPM in D major, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A soft female vocalist sings tender and breathy, close to the microphone in the verses, then clear, emotional and soaring in the chorus with warm harmonies above, holding the echo words long. A fingerpicked acoustic guitar, a warm melodic bass and a wooden box drum carry the verses, a violin plays long notes and answers the vocal phrases, a distant foghorn sounds between sections, and the final chorus opens into a full band with a key change. Nostalgic, bittersweet mood of a foggy island town, a tram along the coast, a doughnut split in half, and echoes that stay. Ends on the guitar fading with waves and a distant foghorn. Warm, natural, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, Cantonese, English vocals, rap, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, heavy distortion, screaming, autotune
