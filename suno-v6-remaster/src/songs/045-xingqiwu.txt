--- STYLE
Hyper playful Chinese trap-pop with chiptune bounce at 134 BPM in F major, rapped and shouted in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist raps with a cheeky, bratty, fast-talking flow in the verses, then chants the chorus with a goofy, elastic, sing-song voice and a crowd of kids answering each line. A bouncy 808 bass, snappy claps, crisp hi-hats and a bright chiptune lead carry the verses, silly cartoon sound effects pop between lines, and the chorus adds stacked gang shouts and a catchy whistle hook. Mischievous, restless mood of a student counting down to Friday, snacks, games and mecha model kits. Ends on a cartoon boing and a laugh. Bright, punchy, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, female lead vocal, ballad, orchestral strings, heavy metal, lo-fi, smooth jazz, autotune
