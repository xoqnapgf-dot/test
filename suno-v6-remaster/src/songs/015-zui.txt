--- STYLE
Heavy Chinese alt-metal with rapped verses at 124 BPM in D minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist raps in a low, gritty, spoken-shouted voice in the verses, then roars and sings with raw sustained high notes in the chorus while a gang of voices shouts echoes behind him. Down-tuned chugging guitars, a heavy bass and tight punchy drums drive the verses, a dark synth drone hangs underneath, and the chorus adds wide distorted power chords, crash cymbals and layered shouted crowd vocals. Guilty, weary and furious mood about inherited original sin and a city drunk on desire. Ends on a final heavy chord with feedback. Punchy, dark, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, female lead vocal, ballad, acoustic folk, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, lo-fi, comedy, autotune
