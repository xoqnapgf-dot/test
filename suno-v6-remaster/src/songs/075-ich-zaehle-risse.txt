--- STYLE
Intimate German indie ballad at 70 BPM in F minor, sung in German. A soft male baritone sings low, tired and honest, close to the microphone and nearly spoken in the verses, then opening into a quiet, aching and sustained chorus with a faint harmony. A felt piano and a low cello carry the whole song, a soft brushed kick and a warm bass enter in the second verse, and the final chorus adds strings and a gentle swell of drums. Quiet, bittersweet mood of counting cracks in a wall, a coat still hanging beside him, a cold coffee, and finally letting go and becoming enough for himself. Ends on the piano alone with a long cello note. Warm, close, vocal-forward mix.
