--- STYLE
Elegant Chinese-style (guofeng) pop ballad at 72 BPM in A minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A female voice sings delicate, silvery and emotional, with a slow shimmer of vibrato at the ends of phrases, soft and breathy in the verses and opening into a luminous, sustained chorus with layered harmonies. A plucked guzheng and a soft pipa carry the verses over a warm pad, an erhu line sings between phrases, a gentle kick and a deep bass enter in the chorus, and a string section and a distant choir swell around the vocal. Wistful, romantic and cosmic mood of stealing fire from the afterlife, starlight, falling galaxies and a love song that echoes forever. Ends on the guzheng alone with a long shimmering note. Spacious, polished, vocal-forward mix.
