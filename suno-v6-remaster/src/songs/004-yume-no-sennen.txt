--- STYLE
Atmospheric cinematic Japanese alternative rock at 86 BPM that builds slowly from a quiet verse to an anthem. A female alto sings in Japanese, hushed and airy in the verses, then clear, wide and soaring in the chorus with sustained line endings. A clean arpeggiated electric guitar and a low cello carry the verses, a brushed kick and soft toms enter in the pre-climb, and the chorus opens with layered harmonies, driving drums, distorted guitars and a string section. Mystical mood of an ancient wind, a dragon's shadow and a thousand-year night under the stars, lonely yet determined. Ends with the guitar arpeggio alone and one long reverb tail. Spacious, vocal-forward mix.
