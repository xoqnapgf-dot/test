--- STYLE
Brash Chinese hip-hop with pop-punk guitars at 100 BPM in G minor, rapped and sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist raps with a cocky, bratty, rhythmic flow in the verses, then shouts and sings the chorus with an arrogant swagger and a crowd of gang vocals. A bouncing bass, punchy live drums with a snappy snare and a gritty detuned guitar loop carry the verses, a siren-like synth stab accents the beat, and the chorus adds crunchy power chords, handclaps and stadium shouts. Cocky, defiant mood of a self-declared strongest man crushing every rival like a police officer chasing a wanted criminal. Ends on a siren and one power chord. Punchy, bright, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, female lead vocal, ballad, lo-fi, orchestral strings, smooth jazz, autotune, country
