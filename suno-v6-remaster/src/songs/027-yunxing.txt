--- STYLE
Ethereal cosmic post-rock and dream pop at 68 BPM in F-sharp minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A female voice sings airy, weightless and tender, close to the microphone and almost whispered in the verses, then rising into a vast, glowing and sustained chorus where the single dash words are held long. A clean guitar with long shimmering delay and a soft felt piano carry the verses, a deep warm pad and slow brushed drums enter in the bridge, and the chorus adds a swelling string section, tremolo guitars, big reverb drums and layered ghostly harmonies. Lonely, awed mood of a star falling like a single tear through the sky and a feather freed from reality. Ends on the guitar delay fading into stars. Spacious, vocal-forward mix with deep reverb.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, rap, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, heavy distortion, screaming, comedy, upbeat pop, Cantonese

[This is a Mandarin song]

[Intro | 8 bars, clean guitar with long shimmering delay, a soft felt piano joins on bar five, a deep pad breathes underneath, instrumental only]

[Verse 1 | airy weightless female voice, almost whispered, guitar delay and piano only, one breath per line, slightly behind the beat]
星，
是流浪在天际的一滴海，

亦不知凡尘的苦痛，

它不知自己是谁，
不知众生意欲何为，
永远飘流，像一滴热泪。

那道门不再关闭。
