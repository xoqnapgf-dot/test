--- STYLE
Melancholic Mandopop rock ballad at 70 BPM in E minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A female alto sings in a worn, honest and slightly husky voice, close to the microphone in the verses, then opening into a wide, aching and sustained chorus with long held line endings. A fingerpicked acoustic guitar and a soft piano carry the verses, a low string pad and a warm bass enter in the second verse, and the chorus adds a live drum kit, strummed guitars, a cello line and layered backing harmonies. Lonely, theatrical mood of a performer wearing a mask under the spotlight, strings pulling her around like a puppet, silver hair at the window at dawn. Ends on the acoustic guitar alone with one long ringing note. Warm, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, rap, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, male lead vocal, heavy distortion, comedy, autotune

[This is a Mandarin song]

[Intro | 8 bars, a fingerpicked acoustic guitar alone, a soft piano answers on bar five, faint stage-curtain room tone, instrumental only]

[Verse 1 | part 1 of 2, worn honest female voice, close-mic, guitar and piano only, one breath per line, slightly behind the beat]
琴声渐淡，
困于名为家的羁绊，
暮退月光漫，
如影的春色终涣散，
心中波澜，
激荡又刹那间虚幻，
深藏的遗憾，
成无人角落的轻叹。

[Verse 1 | part 2 of 2, same voice, string pad and warm bass enter, a little more strained and bitter]
舞台上，
麻木机械应唱，
任由提线引我旋转，
谁知我面具之下是怎样。

[Chorus | wide aching sustained voice, drums and strummed guitars enter, cello answers each pair of lines, backing harmonies in the last two lines]

卸下又披上伪装，
心若烬灰只剩荒唐，
寻不见我本相，
银丝悄然透入窗，
真心虚伪都不可计量，
抱着吉他独坐至天亮。

[Verse 2 | part 1 of 2, back to the worn honest voice, guitar and piano with a light bass, looking back, fragile]

徘徊在无声中倾诉，
独自背负，
受不属于我的错误，
璀璨的谢幕，
终是化作我的坟墓。

[Verse 2 | part 2 of 2, voice rising, drums return softly, strings swell, ending with a hopeful line]
再奔赴，
忽听琴声回荡，
总有风泪交织念想，
但愿每次相见都是安详。

[Chorus | same as the first chorus, a little stronger, same melody]
