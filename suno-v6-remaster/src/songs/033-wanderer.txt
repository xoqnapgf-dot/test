--- STYLE
Epic space rock ballad at 92 BPM in A minor, sung in English. A male baritone-tenor sings warm, weary and heartfelt, intimate and a little hoarse in the verses, then wide, aching and sustained in the chorus with layered harmonies. A clean chorus-drenched electric guitar, a soft piano and a deep melodic bass carry the verses, a slow engine-like rumble rolls underneath, and the chorus adds a wall of reverb guitars, big cinematic drums, a string orchestra and a soaring lead guitar. Nostalgic, bittersweet mood of an old spaceship and her wanderers adrift in the cosmic sea, hope and despair waltzing toward a distant shore. Ends on the guitar echo fading into an engine hum. Wide, cinematic, vocal-forward mix.
