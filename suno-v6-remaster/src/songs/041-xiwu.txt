--- STYLE
Aggressive kung-fu cinematic Chinese hip-hop with rock edges at 98 BPM in E minor, rapped and shouted in Mandarin Chinese. A male rapper delivers a cocky, rhythmic, controlled flow in the verses with sharp breaths between phrases, then shouts the chorus with raw, sustained fighting spirit and gang vocals behind him. Booming 808 bass, punchy live drums, taiko hits, a sharp erhu stab and a plucked guzheng riff carry the verses, with martial-arts mouth sounds between the move names, and the chorus adds crunchy guitars, deep war drums and crowd shouts. Arrogant, fierce prodigy mood of a boy who mastered martial arts at three and now commands the heavens. Ends on a gong hit and silence. Punchy, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, female lead vocal, ballad, lo-fi, comedy, smooth jazz, autotune, country
