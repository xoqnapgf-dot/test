--- STYLE
Ethereal cinematic dream-pop ballad at 72 BPM in D minor. A female alto sings in English, hushed and intimate in the verses, then wide, aching and sustained in the chorus with long held line endings and layered harmonies. A soft felt piano and a shimmering reverb guitar carry the verses, a slow brushed kick and warm bass enter in the second half, and the chorus opens into strings, big reverb drums and a distant choir. Reverent and romantic mood of someone kneeling before a lover's eyes, brighter than stars, with a lighthouse glowing over a sea of ash. Ends on the piano alone with one long string note. Vocal-forward, wide cinematic mix.

Your heart-fire, both so tender and so bright,
It overflows your soul with gentle light.
It blooms upon the world's most lonely peak,
A god once glanced, and made Prometheus weak.
