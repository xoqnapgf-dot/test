--- STYLE
Dark chamber art-pop with a slow trip-hop beat at 76 BPM in C minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A female alto sings low and dreamy, close to the microphone, half-whispered in the verses and opening into a fuller, slightly bitter voice in the chorus, with a hummed second voice answering her phrases. A prepared piano and a low cello carry the verses, a dusty brushed kick and snare fade in under the bridge, and the chorus adds warm sub bass, a wide string pad and a sharp cymbal swell. Eerie, elegant and claustrophobic mood of a world locked inside a picture frame, wet paint and an endless night. Ends on the piano with one last cello note. Vocal-forward mix with deep reverb.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, rap, EDM drop, heavy distortion, upbeat dance beat, autotune, children choir, male lead vocal
