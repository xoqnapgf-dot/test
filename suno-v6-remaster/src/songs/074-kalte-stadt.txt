--- STYLE
Cold driving post-punk with shoegaze reverb at 126 BPM in E minor, sung in German. A female vocalist sings close to the microphone, detached and cool in the verses, whispering into a belt in the pre-chorus, then driving and soaring in the chorus and emotional in the final climax. A cold driving bassline leads the song, a mechanical drum machine and a sparse shimmer guitar carry the intro, sharp guitar stabs answer the drums in the instrumental sections, and the chorus adds a wall of sharp guitars with shoegaze reverb creeping in. Lonely, urban mood of a woman walking an empty wet city of cold windows and unanswered phones, wanting out but staying. Ends with the bass and shimmer guitar fading together as the drum machine dissolves. Cold, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, English vocals, ballad, acoustic folk, country, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, orchestral strings
