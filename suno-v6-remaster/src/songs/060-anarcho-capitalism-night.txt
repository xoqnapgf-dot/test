--- STYLE
Cold driving post-punk at 130 BPM in E minor, sung in Japanese. A male vocalist sings deadpan and detached in the verses, almost spoken, then in the chorus rises to a clear, defiant, sustained voice with a distant layered double. A driving melodic bass guitar with a chorus effect carries the whole song, a cold repetitive drum beat keeps a steady motorik pulse, an echoing guitar with feedback and sharp angular stabs rides on top, and the chorus adds wider guitars, a bright cold synth line and a heavier kick. Rational, rebellious, nocturnal mood of a free individual who owns his own body and signs contracts at the bottom of the night. Ends with the pulsating bass fading out alone. Cold, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, English vocals, ballad, acoustic folk, country, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, orchestral strings
