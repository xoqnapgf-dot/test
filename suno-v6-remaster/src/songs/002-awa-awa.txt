--- STYLE
Melancholic Japanese dream pop at 94 BPM with glassy bubble-like synth plucks and shimmering reverb guitar. A breathy female vocalist sings in Japanese close to the microphone, almost whispered in the verses and opening into layered harmonies in the chorus, unhurried and slightly behind the beat. A soft sub bass and a light sidechained pad breathe underneath, a dry kick and rim click enter in the chorus, and a music-box melody twinkles between phrases. Nostalgic and bittersweet, like watching soap bubbles drift away in morning light. Ends on a fading synth bubble. Vocal-forward, airy, spacious mix.
--- EXCLUDE
male vocals, rap, screaming, heavy distortion, metal, trap hi-hats, orchestral brass, English vocals, autotune
