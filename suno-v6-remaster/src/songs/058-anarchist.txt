--- STYLE
Chinese hardcore punk with shouted rap at 150 BPM in E minor, shouted and rapped in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist barks the verses in a loud, hoarse, sarcastic rapped delivery with a clear breath after every line, then screams the chorus in a ragged, sustained shout with a crowd of gang vocals chanting the title. Fast distorted power chords, a driving distorted bass and fast live drums with a snare on every backbeat carry the verses, the chorus switches to a half-time feel with huge chords, crash cymbals and gang shouts, and a siren wails in the gaps. Furious, mocking mood of a self-declared anarchist who rejects every ruler and law. Ends on a distorted chord, feedback and a crowd roar. Raw, loud, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, female lead vocal, ballad, acoustic folk, orchestral strings, country, smooth jazz, autotune
