--- STYLE
Theatrical Chinese-style (guofeng) electronic rock opera at 100 BPM in E minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist sings with a bold, flamboyant, slightly husky voice in a stage-like delivery, mocking and relaxed in the verses, then wide, proud and sustained in the chorus with a crowd chanting the key line. A slow plucked guzheng, a distant erhu and a soft electronic pad open the song, a deep sub bass and tight drums carry the verses, the chorus adds crunchy guitars, taiko hits, brass-like synth stabs and chanting voices, and the bridge breaks into a slow Peking-opera-style recitation. Defiant, humorous, chaotic mood of a monkey king smashing the heavenly palace and a world turning inside out. Ends with the guzheng and erhu fading and a soft electronic pad dissolving. Wide, punchy, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, female lead vocal, ballad, acoustic folk, country, trap hi-hats, orchestral strings, autotune
