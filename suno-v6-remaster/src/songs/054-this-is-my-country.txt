--- STYLE
Gritty protest hip-hop with a soulful hook at 90 BPM in C minor, rapped and sung in English. A male rapper delivers a stern, weary, clear flow in the verses with weight on the first word of each line, then sings the chorus in a rough, aching, sustained voice with a few gang voices behind. A dusty boom-bap kick and snare, a slow soul guitar line and a deep warm bass carry the verses, a lonely trumpet plays long notes between phrases, and the chorus adds a gospel-like organ, a wide pad and stacked harmonies. Bitter, serious mood of a nation of gunshots, medical bills, money in the halls and empty slogans. Ends on the lonely trumpet fading. Raw, warm, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, ballad only piano, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, country, heavy metal, comedy, autotune, upbeat pop
