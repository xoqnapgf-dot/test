--- STYLE
Epic Japanese symphonic power metal with folk touches at 132 BPM in D minor. A powerful male tenor sings in Japanese, low and restrained in the verses, then heroic and open-throated in the chorus, holding the last note of every line. Palm-muted rhythm guitars and double-kick drums drive the verses, taiko drums and a low shakuhachi answer the vocal phrases, and in the chorus a male choir, twin lead guitars and a string orchestra open wide. Dark mythic mood of a dragon crying over night mountains, bittersweet yet triumphant. The song ends on a held vocal note and one final taiko hit. Polished wide stereo mix, vocal-forward balance.
--- EXCLUDE
English vocals, female lead vocal, rap, autotune, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, acoustic ballad, lo-fi, children choir, spoken intro, reggae
