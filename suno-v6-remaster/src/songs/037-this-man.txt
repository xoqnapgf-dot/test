--- STYLE
Cold new-wave and post-punk with a driving 1980s synth sheen at 128 BPM in A minor, sung in English. A deep baritone male voice sings detached and deadpan in the verses, like a distant narrator, then switches to shouting, questioning, wide and anthemic in the chorus. A driving melodic bassline carries the song, a mechanical drum machine beat and cold atmospheric synth pads sit underneath, layered 80s analog synthesizers undulate in the chorus, and a sharp chorus-pedal guitar adds angular stabs. Lonely, prophetic mood of a man on a mountain peak holding the sun's light for a blind valley that cannot see it. Ends with a synth solo, then the bassline slowly fading out alone. Punchy, cold, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, acoustic folk, country, ballad, orchestral strings, rap, opera, comedy, trap hi-hats
