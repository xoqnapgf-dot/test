--- STYLE
Cosmic indie-electronic rock with glitchy textures at 100 BPM in D minor, sung in English. A male tenor sings thoughtful and slightly haunted, close to the microphone and half-spoken in the verses, then open, aching and sustained in the chorus with layered harmonies. A pulsing analog bass, a tight programmed kick, a clean chorus-drenched guitar and tiny glitch fragments carry the verses, and the chorus adds big reverb drums, a wide synth pad, a bright lead guitar and a rising choir of voices. Philosophical, vast mood of a being born from the void, flipping a coin with fate and asking who he is in the universe. Ends on a single coin-toss sound and the pad fading. Polished, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, rap, country, acoustic folk, screaming, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, heavy metal
