--- STYLE
Dark neon synth-rock at 112 BPM with a nocturnal city-pop sourness. A male vocalist sings in Japanese with a smoky, slightly drunk baritone, restrained and spoken-like in the verses, shouting and ragged in the chorus. Driving analog bass, tight live drums with a gated snare, pulsing neon synth arpeggios and an angular clean guitar carry the verses, while the chorus adds distorted rhythm guitars and gang vocals. Hazy, decadent, self-destructive mood of walking a crowded city at night, free and hopeless at once. Ends with the synth arpeggio looping alone and cutting off. Wide stereo mix with punchy low end.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, orchestral strings, acoustic folk, reggae, ballad, children choir, English vocals, lo-fi hiss, opera
