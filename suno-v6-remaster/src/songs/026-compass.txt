--- STYLE
Dark ritual rock with cinematic Eastern flavor at 104 BPM in A minor, sung in English. A male baritone sings low, solemn and prophetic in the verses, almost chanted, then wide, ancient and fierce in the chorus with a deep choir doubling the key lines. Deep ritual toms, a growling bass and a bowed erhu-like string carry the verses, crunchy detuned guitars and big reverb drums join in the chorus, and low brass swells and distant temple bells hang in the air. Solemn, dangerous mood of a dragon's compass leading souls through five elements toward an immortal curse. Ends on a single temple bell and a fading drone. Dark, wide, vocal-forward mix.
