--- STYLE
Epic Chinese-style rock with electronic edges at 96 BPM in D minor, sung and rapped in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist sings with a warm, clear, slightly husky tenor in the verses and chorus, holding long notes at line ends, and rhythmically raps the middle verse in a low, steady, confident flow. A guzheng arpeggio, a soft erhu line and a deep sub bass carry the verses, punchy live drums and crunchy electric guitars join in the chorus, and the chorus opens with strings, a distant choir and a bright dizi flute. Mythic, adventurous mood of chasing an old legend through golden light, deserts and rivers, loneliness turning into an inexhaustible song. Ends on the guzheng alone with a long ringing note. Polished, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, female lead vocal, piano ballad, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, autotune, country
