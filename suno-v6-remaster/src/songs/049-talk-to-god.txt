Cosmic conversational rap-rock with a cinematic arena build at 108 BPM in A minor, sung and rapped in English. A male vocalist tells the story in a wry, wide-eyed, half-spoken voice in the verses, speeding into sarcastic rap in the second verse, then shouts the chorus with a questioning crowd of gang vocals answering every line.
