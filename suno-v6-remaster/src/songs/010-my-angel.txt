--- STYLE
Haunting dark synth-pop ballad at 70 BPM in F-sharp minor. A female voice sings in English, soft and fragile in the verses, then aching and full in the chorus with long sustained line endings and a breath of vibrato at the ends. A soft piano with gentle strings opens the song, a deep warm synth bass and a slow muted kick carry the verses, and the chorus adds big reverb drums, wide strings and a glowing pad. Cold, tender and longing mood of someone waiting forever in a frozen land for an angel who left. Ends on the piano with one fading string note. Vocal-forward, spacious mix.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, rap, EDM drop, screaming, country, fast tempo, trap hi-hats, comedy, autotune
