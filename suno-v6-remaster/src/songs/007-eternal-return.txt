--- STYLE
Cosmic synth-pop with an anthemic indie-rock lift at 108 BPM in E minor. A warm male tenor sings in English, intimate and clear in the verses, then open-chested and soaring in the chorus with long held vowels at the ends of phrases. Pulsing analog bass, a shimmering octave arpeggio and tight live drums drive the verses, and the chorus adds big reverb snare, wide supersaw pads, bright clean guitar and layered group harmonies. Romantic, vast and hopeful mood of two souls reborn again and again through stars and time. Ends on the arpeggio fading into a long shimmering pad. Polished wide stereo mix, vocal-forward.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, rap, growl, metal distortion, country, lo-fi, spoken word, trap hi-hats, orchestral brass
