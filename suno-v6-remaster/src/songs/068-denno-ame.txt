--- STYLE
Cold bass-led Japanese cyberpunk synth-pop at 102 BPM in C minor, sung in Japanese. A female vocalist sings languid, breathy and detached in the verses with minimal backing, then fuller and cold in the chorus where her restraint breaks, with a wailing edge on the final chorus. A solo melodic bass with a chorus effect leads the song, a hypnotic synth bass enters in the second verse, tight drums and a glitchy hi-hat build in the pre-chorus, and the chorus adds a prominent driving bass, cold anthemic synth pads and wide guitars. Lonely, rainy mood of a girl with a prosthetic hand walking a neon city at three a.m. asking whether she is alive or just data. Ends on the bass line repeating alone while glitch noise and rain fade. Dark, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, English vocals, ballad, acoustic folk, country, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, orchestral strings
