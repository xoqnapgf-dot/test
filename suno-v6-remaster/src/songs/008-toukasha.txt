--- STYLE
Cinematic Mandopop rock ballad at 68 BPM in B minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male tenor sings with a tender, devout, slightly husky tone, intimate and nearly spoken in the verses, then rising to a wide, aching and sustained chorus where he holds the end of each line. A felt piano and a low cello carry the verses, soft brushed drums and a warm bass enter in the second half, and the chorus opens into a string orchestra, big reverb drums, a distant choir and a slow soaring electric guitar. Reverent, romantic and vast mood, a lover as a sacred light above an endless sea of ash, like a lighthouse in the dark. Ends on the piano alone with one long string note. Vocal-forward, wide cinematic mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, rap, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, autotune, female lead vocal, fast tempo, shouting

[Intro | 8 bars, felt piano alone with long reverb, a low cello joins on bar five, a distant wind texture, instrumental only]

[Verse 1 | part 1 of 4, male tenor, intimate and nearly spoken, piano only, one breath per line, behind the beat]
若非直面你的瞳孔，
我几乎忘却自己双眼的散光，
夜晚的车灯汇聚，
光团在身前发散，
若星海闪耀，
有如银河坠陨人间，
可它们却不及与你对视之刹那的万分之一。

[Verse 1 | part 2 of 4, same hushed voice, cello holds long notes under the piano]
星辰是拙劣的模仿者，
于你睁眼时自惭形秽，
大海借鉴了它们的浩瀚，
天空模仿着它们的清澈，
就连宇宙本身，
也在臣服中效仿它们的深邃，
圣洁光芒充盈尘世、洗涤污秽、抚慰我心。

[Verse 1 | part 3 of 4, voice slightly warmer, soft brushed drums and a warm bass enter, slow and steady]
当万物终焉，
天地将借由你的瞳色浸染自身，
待冰河逆流，
众生将裹挟你的温度冉冉重生，
你的心火炽热而温柔。

[Verse 1 | part 4 of 4, voice lifting, strings swell slowly, the last line is sung with awe and a held final word]
溢出灵魂，
流向每一条干涸的水道，
又悄然绽放在世界之巅，
众神中的一员匆匆一瞥，
于是高加索山有了一位窃火者。

[Break | band stops, one beat of silence, singer breathes in]

[Chorus | wide, aching and sustained voice, full string orchestra and big reverb drums enter, distant choir behind, electric guitar soars slowly, line endings held long]
我是最忠实的信徒，

觐见永恒中唯一的奇迹，
跪求虚无中缥缈的怜悯，
若生命有意义，
那它应受你应允，
若岁月有痕迹，
则你为其流动之痕迹，
当我挣脱归墟的漩涡，
脱离终焉的寂灭，
你是无尽灰烬海之上唯一的亮光，
似灯塔指引迷茫的船只，
如引力叙说既定的方向。

[Verse 2 | part 1 of 2, back to the hushed tender voice, piano and cello only, drums gone, intimate and slow]
你知道，
当我看向晚霞，
并不只是在看晚霞，
而是借由这些天穹在宇宙的幽灵，
搭建一条与你相见的桥梁，
你也知道，
当我想你，
也不仅仅是在是思念你，
而是同样屈服于无穷概率与不可达之领域间，
你存在于此刻的证明。

[Verse 2 | part 2 of 2, strings rise and the voice swells with each line, brushed drums return, building toward the climax]
当群星啜泣，
当夕阳凝望，
当天幕披上名为“黄昏”的薄纱，
霞光会如血海激荡在穹顶，
而那粉色的，
会化作心底最轻柔的内衬。
