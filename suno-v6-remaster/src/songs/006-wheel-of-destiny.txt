--- STYLE
Hypnotic dark synth-pop with dream-pop haze at 84 BPM in A minor. A female alto sings in English, hushed and breathy in the verses and lifting into a wide, airy chorus with layered harmonies, unhurried and slightly behind the beat. A slowly rotating analog arpeggio, a deep warm bass and soft clock-like percussion carry the verses, and the chorus adds a wide pad, a gated snare and a shimmering lead synth that circles back on itself. Dreamlike and fatalistic, like a great wheel turning in the dark. Ends on the arpeggio slowing down until it stops. Vocal-forward mix with spacious reverb.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, rap, screaming, heavy metal guitars, trap hi-hats, country, acoustic folk, orchestral brass, children choir
