--- STYLE
Dark cinematic Chinese hip-hop with industrial rock edges at 92 BPM in F minor, rapped in Mandarin Chinese. A male rapper delivers a low, gritty, controlled flow in the verses, almost spoken and menacing, then shouts the hook in the chorus with gang vocals behind him. A distorted sub-bass 808, a slow heavy kick and snare, a gritty industrial guitar loop and a cold string drone carry the verses, and the chorus adds wide distorted guitars, crowd shouts and crashing cymbals. Lonely and vengeful mood of a hunter walking a sleepless neon city at night, scarred and unbroken. Ends with the guitar loop alone and one last 808 hit. Dark, punchy, vocal-forward mix.
