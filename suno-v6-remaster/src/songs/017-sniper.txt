--- STYLE
Dark cinematic trap-rock at 140 BPM with a half-time feel, rapped and sung in English. A male vocalist raps with a confident, cold, menacing flow in the verses, almost whispered and precise like a sniper's breath, then sings and shouts the chorus with a gritty, sustained voice and gang vocals behind. Booming 808 bass, a slow heavy snare, sparse hi-hat rolls and a gritty detuned guitar loop carry the verses, a distant gunshot echo and a cold synth pad hang in the air, and the chorus adds distorted power chords and crowd shouts. Cold, lethal, legendary mood of a ghost hidden in forest leaves and desert sand. Ends with one gunshot echo and silence. Punchy, wide, vocal-forward mix.
