--- STYLE
Dreamy Mandopop R&B with a hazy electronic pulse at 80 BPM in A minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A female vocalist sings airy and tender, close to the microphone, with unhurried relaxed phrasing and a slight breath at the ends of lines, floating into soft layered harmonies in the chorus. A warm Rhodes piano, a rounded sub bass and a slow soft snare carry the verses, a glassy synth pad and a faint guitar echo open up the bridge, and the chorus adds a gentle four-on-the-floor kick and shimmering vocal doubles. Wistful, intimate mood of someone remembering a smile in a foggy rainbow-colored memory. Ends on the Rhodes alone with the pad fading. Vocal-forward, soft spacious mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, rap, screaming, heavy distortion, metal, fast tempo, trap hi-hats, male lead vocal, autotune
