--- STYLE
Cosmic dream-pop with glitchy electronica at 90 BPM in B-flat minor, sung in English. A female vocalist sings airy and floating, close to the microphone, soft and spoken-like in the verses, then bright, wide and shimmering in the chorus with layered harmonies that echo like reflections. Glassy bell-like synth plucks, a warm sub bass and a soft rolling kick carry the verses, tiny glitch fragments sparkle like falling shards, and the chorus adds a wide supersaw pad, a shimmering arpeggio and crisp hi-hats. Mystical, bittersweet mood of an eternal mirror at the edge of the cosmos cracking into endless stars. Ends on a single bell tone fading into reverb. Vocal-forward, spacious, glittering mix.
--- EXCLUDE
male lead vocal, rap, heavy distortion, metal, country, acoustic folk, screaming, trap hi-hats, comedy
