--- STYLE
Furious alt-metal with hardcore shouts and a rapped-shouted verse delivery at 132 BPM in C minor, sung in English. A male vocalist spits the verses in a hoarse, gritty, half-spoken rage, then screams and sings the chorus with a ragged, sustained, tormented voice and gang vocals shouting the command words. Down-tuned chugging guitars, a pounding bass and heavy live drums carry the verses, a screaming lead guitar flares between lines, and the chorus adds wide distorted chords, crash cymbals and a wall of crowd shouts. Raw, unhinged mood of someone tearing open his own cold core and declaring himself the ruler of his own world. Ends on a final guitar chord with feedback. Punchy, aggressive, vocal-forward mix.
