--- STYLE
Chinese-style (guofeng) rock with a rapped-sung swagger at 108 BPM in D minor, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist sings with a free, carefree, slightly husky voice, relaxed and half-spoken in the verses, then wide, bold and sustained in the chorus with gang shouts behind him. A bamboo flute and a plucked pipa carry the verses over a deep bass and a steady live kick, the chorus adds crunchy electric guitars, taiko-style big drums, an erhu line and a crowd of shouting voices. Lonely, unbound swordsman mood of a god descending a mountain, wine in hand, moonlight and snow, laughing at the courts below. Ends on one pipa strum and a flute note fading out. Punchy, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, English vocals, female lead vocal, ballad, EDM drop, trap hi-hats, comedy, autotune, country
