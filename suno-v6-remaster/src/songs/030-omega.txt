--- STYLE
Motorik indie post-punk with new-wave synths at 124 BPM in E minor, sung in English. A male vocalist delivers a detached, deadpan monotone in the verses, half-spoken with dry wit, then becomes anthemic and increasingly frantic in the chorus, ending in unhinged shouts. A driving melodic bassline, a tight drum machine beat and a clean shimmering guitar with heavy delay carry the verses, short synth bleeps and percussive hits accent the rhythm, and the chorus adds open-chord guitars, a bright synth lead and gang vocals. Absurd, cosmic and hypnotic mood of a trickster god, five dollars and a crack in space and time. Ends with the bass and drum machine looping and then cutting out abruptly. Punchy, dry, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, country, ballad, orchestral strings, acoustic folk, opera, smooth jazz, trap hi-hats, lo-fi hiss
