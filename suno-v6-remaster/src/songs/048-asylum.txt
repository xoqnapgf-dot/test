--- STYLE
Experimental avant-garde alt-rock with dark trip-hop pulses at 96 BPM in B minor, half-whispered and half-sung in English. A male vocalist delivers a broken, twitchy, half-whispered flow in the verses like a patient confiding in a corridor, then erupts into a frenzied melodic hook with screams and giggles in the chorus. A twisted, warped, bent-string distorted guitar, a rubbery sub bass and a stuttering broken drum loop carry the verses, creepy music-box and radio-static textures flicker in the gaps, and the chorus adds wide fuzz guitars, panicked shouts and echoing voices. Surreal, claustrophobic, darkly funny mood inside a white-tiled asylum, a dog-faced doctor and a blue door that cannot be touched. Ends on the music box slowing to a stop. Raw, eerie, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, ballad, orchestral strings, country, smooth jazz, polished pop, acoustic folk, comedy rap, autotune
