--- STYLE
Angry Chinese rap-rock with nu-metal weight at 112 BPM in G minor, rapped and shouted in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist raps in a hoarse, furious, ragged voice in the first verse, switches to a humorous, cocky and inspiring flow in the second verse, and roars the chorus in an exciting shouted chant with a crowd answering. Down-tuned chugging guitars, a pounding bass and heavy drums carry the verses, a bursting bass drop with sharp high-frequency noise hits in the break, and the chorus adds wide distorted chords, crash cymbals and a huge crowd of gang vocals. Rebellious, theatrical mood of a boy raised on cartoon heroes who decides to change the world, with a warrior's roar and a joke. Ends on a final power chord and crowd cheer. Punchy, aggressive, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
Cantonese, female lead vocal, ballad, acoustic folk, country, lo-fi, smooth jazz, autotune, orchestral strings
