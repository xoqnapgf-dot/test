--- STYLE
Emotional Chinese alt-rock ballad at 84 BPM in B minor that builds from a hush to a roar, sung in Mandarin Chinese. A male vocalist sings raw, honest and slightly hoarse, close to the microphone and almost spoken in the verses, then wide, broken and sustained in the chorus with a second voice echoing the key words. A clean electric guitar with long delay and a soft piano carry the verses, a warm bass and brushed drums enter in the second half, and the chorus adds a heavy snare, wall-of-sound guitars, strings and layered backing vocals. Proud, solitary and aching mood of a gifted loner who calls his loneliness an incurable disease. Ends on the piano with one distant guitar note. Vocal-forward, wide cinematic mix.
