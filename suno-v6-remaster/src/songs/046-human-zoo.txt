--- STYLE
Dark carnival industrial rap-metal at 126 BPM in D minor, shouted and rapped in English. A male vocalist barks the verses in a hoarse, theatrical, sneering ringmaster voice, then shouts the chorus with a chanting crowd of gang vocals, spitting the final break like a speech. Heavy distorted guitar riffs, a thick bass, punchy live drums and a mechanical circus calliope loop carry the verses, DJ scratches and sirens cut between phrases, and the chorus adds wide distorted chords, crash cymbals and shouted crowd responses. Grotesque, satirical mood of a zoo where the humans are the beasts, with monkeys, a bald hippo and a squirrel in a cage. Ends on a giant door slam and a laugh. Punchy, wide, vocal-forward mix.
--- EXCLUDE
female lead vocal, ballad, acoustic folk, country, smooth jazz, lo-fi, comedy pop, autotune, orchestral strings
