FIELD GUIDE · SIX WORLDS

A little map of the cube

Six independent model families meet on one turning cube. Every square is a different capability—not a score, endorsement, or fixed leaderboard.

Move through the field

↗Turn a layer
Drag a tile in a straight line. The tile’s row or column chooses the slice.
⤾Orbit the camera
Drag empty space. Right-drag also orbits from over the cube.
⌕Zoom
Use the wheel or a two-finger pinch. Double-click resets the view.
UKeyboard turns
U R F D L B and M E S; Shift makes the inverse, Alt makes a half-turn. X Y Z rotate the whole cube.

NOT A RANKING

Six separate worlds

Capabilities are dated snapshots of notable public product and model claims. They overlap, change quickly, and are not a controlled comparison.

How to read the small details

The tiny route couriers, mirror shard, and blue plate are qualified references, not verdicts. Sources, snapshot dates, survey limits, benchmark scope, and the OpenRouter traffic caveat are listed in the project README and research notes.

Everything is generated locally in the browser. The scene does not send cube moves, model names, or analytics to a service.
